Get help

Found a bug, have a question about the Keeper's Charter, or want to suggest a recipe? Open a support request. No account details are needed; please don't include personal information.

Common questions

How do I cancel the Keeper's Charter? Open iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › PrepQuest. Recipes you've already received stay in your Guild Board.

How do I restore my subscription on a new phone? In PrepQuest, open Tavern › Preferences & saved progress › Membership › Restore purchases.

How do I move my progress to another device? Turn on iCloud sync in Preferences on both devices, or export a backup file from Preferences and import it on the other device.

Are the nutrition numbers exact? No. They are estimates. Always check ingredient labels for allergens and use a food thermometer.
